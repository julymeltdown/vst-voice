# Mandatory Official Voicebank 01 and Character 01 Validation

These validations are non-optional. Official Voicebank 01 and Character 01 cannot be labelled PASS, ACCEPTED, Beta, Release Candidate, or General Availability until actual contract, recording, rights, clearance, package, installation, and approval evidence exists and its SHA-256 is recorded.

Implementation states are separate from validation results. `SOURCE_READY`, `CI_CONFIGURED`, and `TARGET_BUILD_PASS` do not equal `PASS`.

The authoritative required rows are in `mandatory-validation-matrix.json`. Any mandatory row that is NOT_RUN, BLOCKED, FAIL, missing evidence, or hash-mismatched blocks G5.
