# Phase 13B Acceptance

## Engineering acceptance

Phase 13B engineering passes when dossier parsers, evidence hashing, voicebank inventory audit, character asset audit, deterministic development packaging, G5 gating, CLI, source contracts, and CTest integration pass.

## Product-release acceptance

Engineering completion is not content or legal acceptance. Official Voicebank 01 and Character 01 remain `BLOCKED` until every mandatory row in `mandatory-validation-matrix.json` contains actual `PASS` evidence with matching SHA-256.

The following are mandatory and cannot be waived:

- selected performer and signed contract;
- rights review and directed recording-session evidence;
- complete unit inventory, retake closure, marker QA, and four-renderer listening QA;
- signed `.seambank` and installation receipt;
- final character name and trademark/domain/social clearance;
- artist/IP assignment and source provenance;
- final turnaround, production topology, LODs, expressions, animation, runtime states, and key art;
- final EULA, voicebank licence, product-owner approval, and legal approval;
- all mandatory Phase 12C and Phase 13A target-OS, validator, installer, signing, notarization, and commercial-DAW evidence.

A public-domain demo recording cannot satisfy the performer-contract or directed-recording gates. Current Character 01 concept and blockout assets cannot satisfy final production-asset or IP-clearance gates.
