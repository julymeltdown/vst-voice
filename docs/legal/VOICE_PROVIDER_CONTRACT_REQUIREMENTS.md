# Voice provider contract requirements

Before a package can declare `official=true` and `contractedSinger=true`, retain signed evidence covering:

- performer identity and capacity;
- recording copyright ownership or exclusive commercial licence;
- phoneme cutting, pitch, duration, formant and spectral modification;
- voicebank sale and user-created commercial audio;
- character-linked marketing, performer name/credit and publicity choices;
- territory, term, compensation, audit and update/retake obligations;
- original-sample extraction and redistribution restrictions;
- machine-learning permission or prohibition;
- termination, sell-through, warranties and dispute terms.

Build tooling must never infer contract completion from an internet licence or intake form.

## External Beta acquisition minimum

A Beta acquisition agreement may omit official-character marketing rights, but it must explicitly grant every scope below before any recording is admitted:

- recording and source-sample use in the Project SEAM production workflow;
- editing, segmentation, resampling, marker work, and other transformations required to build the bank;
- redistribution of the transformed samples as a local singing voicebank, including commercial distribution of the bank;
- commercial and non-commercial end-user rendered audio produced with the bank.

The agreement must identify the performer or lawful source owner, the covered sessions, territory, term, withdrawal and retake rules, and the public redacted approval record. A commercial-output permission, a free-use label, or permission to publish ordinary TTS renders does not by itself grant redistribution of source-like samples or a derived singing voicebank.

This document is an acquisition template. Its existence proves that a contract path is feasible; it never proves that a performer, recording, source licence, or release approval has been obtained.
