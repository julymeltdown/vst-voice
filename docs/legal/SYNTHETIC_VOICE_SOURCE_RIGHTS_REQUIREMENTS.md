# Synthetic voice source rights requirements

Procedural synthesis or TTS-derived audio may enter the Project SEAM voicebank workflow only when written evidence covers all four scopes:

- use of the generated source audio and any model-produced samples;
- transformation, segmentation, resampling, and analysis of those samples;
- redistribution of source-like or transformed samples as a singing voicebank;
- commercial and non-commercial end-user audio rendered with that bank.

The review must cover the model licence, service terms, speaker or voice licence, output terms, dataset restrictions that flow into outputs, and any prohibition on competing models, sample libraries, or reusable voice products. Evidence must identify the exact service or model version and the effective terms reviewed.

Commercial output permission is insufficient when reusable sample redistribution or a derived singing bank is absent, ambiguous, or prohibited. A free tier, open demo, public API, or attribution option does not change that rule.

For self-authored procedural synthesis, the producer must record source-code and dependency licences, confirm that no restricted samples or learned voice identity enter the output, and pass the same coverage and independent listening review used for recorded sources.

This document defines an admission test. It is not a licence grant and does not approve any named model, service, generated output, or production asset.
