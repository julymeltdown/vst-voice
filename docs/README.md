## Specifications, protocols and evidence

The [format specifications](formats/) describe the formats accepted by current readers, including older versions. [ADRs](adr/) preserve design decisions. The contract origin roadmap is preserved byte for byte. It cites three unavailable local targets, including a production brief. The [availability record](reviews/HISTORICAL_EVIDENCE_AVAILABILITY_2026-10-11.md) identifies unavailable attachments. The [full-scope plan](plans/2026-09-05-1718-feat-full-scope-beta-go-plan.md) defines U1–U48. [Authoring](authoring/), [brand](brand/), [licensing](licensing/) and [references](references/) cover their respective workflows. An old date on a contract, protocol, rights record or fixture can identify the exact version a consumer requires.

Current design evidence includes the [embedded Cerulean capture](design/captures/README.md). The [native observation kit](implementation/NATIVE_OBSERVATION_KIT_2026-10-12.md) specifies the owner session. Frozen listening packets, test fixtures, source receipts and hash inventories retain their original identities. For earlier development reports, phase summaries or past scan results, use the [retirement and recovery index](history/RETIRED_DOCUMENTS.md).
