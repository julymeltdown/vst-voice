# Character Direction — Phase 1

## Retained directions

### A. Burgundy — primary

Strengths:

- restrained hoodie and canvas-shoe silhouette;
- strongest connection between emo styling and visible seam motif;
- burgundy works as a warm manual-edit/accent color.

Required Phase 2 changes:

- remove dangling pseudo-labels;
- replace generic waveform chest art with a smaller proprietary unit marker;
- reduce chain/hardware detail;
- make repair stitches functional rather than decorative.

### B. Violet — minimal control

Strengths:

- clean 128 px silhouette;
- few accessories;
- useful baseline for testing how little styling is needed.

Required changes:

- avoid reading as generic goth;
- differentiate fabric sections by cut and construction, not decorative stitches;
- preserve ordinary footwear and cardigan proportions.

### C. Teal — software-state experiment

Strengths:

- clear status/accent color;
- strong asymmetry;
- best test of technical sample-index motifs.

Required changes:

- remove combat/techwear bias;
- replace boots with worn canvas shoes;
- shorten layered shirt and reduce tags;
- keep teal as a UI-state accent, not a futuristic costume identity.

## Selection process for Phase 2

1. redraw all three using the same neutral pose and body proportions;
2. remove all unapproved pseudo-text and accessory clutter;
3. conduct 128/64 px silhouette tests;
4. create equivalent low-poly blockouts at the same triangle budget;
5. perform blind fan review without concept labels;
6. select one canonical direction and retain one alternate-costume direction only.
