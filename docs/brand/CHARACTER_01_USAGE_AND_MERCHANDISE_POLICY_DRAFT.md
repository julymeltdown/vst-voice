# Character 01 usage and merchandise policy — draft

This is a design-policy draft, not approval evidence.

## Product use

Allowed product surfaces:

- Welcome and voicebank browser;
- Official Voicebank 01 product card;
- optional Full/Minimal editor dock;
- render complete, warning and error states;
- installer, store, documentation and approved key art.

Disallowed product surfaces:

- covering Piano Roll, Phoneme, Unit, Automation, Waveform or Spectrogram lanes;
- controlling synthesis or routing;
- changing cache identity or exported PCM;
- presenting Character 01 as the human performer;
- microphone, stage or fictional-band biography as canonical identity;
- self-harm imagery or direct `EMO`, `SAD`, `BROKEN`, `DIY` slogans.

## Merchandise

Commercial merchandise remains blocked until name, trademark, IP ownership,
production art and legal approval are complete. Approved merchandise must use
versioned source art and preserve the performer/character separation statement.
