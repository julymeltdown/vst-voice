# Character 01 public-name clearance

`Character 01` is an internal identifier only. No public name is approved.

A candidate may become the public name only after all of the following are
recorded with evidence hashes:

1. spelling and pronunciation in supported locales;
2. trademark searches in planned sales jurisdictions;
3. company, product and entertainment-name conflict review;
4. primary domains and material typo domains;
5. major social handles;
6. marketplace and app-store name conflicts;
7. legal and product-owner approval.

A domain being available is not trademark clearance. A trademark search is not
legal approval. The release dossier remains blocked until all required records
are PASS.
