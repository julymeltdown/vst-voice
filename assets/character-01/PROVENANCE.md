# Character Concept Provenance

## Status

The files under `concepts/` are first-party internal concept explorations generated for Project SEAM during the product-design conversation. The files under `blockouts/` were generated by `tools/character-blockout/generate_blockouts.py` from first-party primitive geometry.

## Per-state runtime art (manifest version 0.2.0)

The six state portraits (`runtime/neutral.ppm`, `focused.ppm`, `rendering.ppm`, `complete.ppm`, `warning.ppm`, `error.ppm`), the six `runtime/mouth-*.ppm` sprites and their masters under `source/states/` are **AI-generated development art**:

- Generated on 2026-09-27 with Codex's built-in image generator from the owner's protagonist reference sheet (`docs/design/references/protagonist-reference-sheet-2026-09-25.png`) and the approved EMO look portrait (`assets/ui-design/emo/portrait.png`). The `neutral` output was then used as a third reference for the other five states so face, hair, outfit, framing and background stay the same while only pose and expression change.
- Prompt summary: the same original character (black messy layered hair with one pale streak, heavy eyeliner, mole under the left eye, charcoal hoodie, striped arm warmers, studded belt), hand-inked monochrome manga rendering with a faint crimson rim light, half-body 2:3 frame with the head in the top two fifths, plain dark charcoal background; one pose/expression per state. Every prompt forbade text, logos, brand marks, stars, band names, watermarks, microphones, instruments and extra people.
- `scripts/build_character_state_art.py` resized the generator outputs to the 640×960 masters and derives the 320×480 runtime PPMs from them. The mouth sprites are the singing face's own pixels with the lips redrawn procedurally by that script. `source/states/sources.json` records the SHA-256 of each generator output and master; the generator outputs are not committed.
- Every output was reviewed by eye before acceptance for identity consistency and for logos, lettering or brand-like marks. None was found. This review does not replace a legal clearance.
- The manifest keeps `developmentOnly: true`. This art is not the final production character and carries the restrictions below.

The EMO/SCENE splash key art made in the same session lives in `assets/ui-design/` and is recorded in that directory's `PROVENANCE.md`.

## SCENE outfit set and eye boxes (manifest version 0.3.0)

The six SCENE-mode portraits (`runtime/scene/*.ppm`), their mouth sprites (`runtime/scene/mouth-*.ppm`) and their 640×960 masters (`source/states/scene/*.png`) are also **AI-generated development art**:

- Generated on 2026-09-27 with Codex's built-in image generator as edits of the committed EMO masters (`source/states/<state>.png`), one edit per state, so pose, framing, face, outfit and background are the EMO state's own. The edit adds hot-pink and cyan streaks through the hair beside the existing pale streak, stacked neon bead bracelets (pink, cyan, lime) on the wrists, and a pink/cyan rim light in place of the crimson one.
- Every edit prompt forbade text, logos, brand marks, stars, band names, watermarks and any change to pose, expression or composition. Each output was compared by eye with its EMO state for identity, pose and framing, and checked for lettering or brand-like marks. None was found. This review does not replace a legal clearance.
- `scripts/build_character_state_art.py import --outfit scene` stores the masters and records their SHA-256 in `source/states/sources.json`; `build` derives the runtime PPMs and the SCENE mouth sprites exactly as for the shared set. The generator outputs are not committed.
- The per-state `eyes` boxes in `manifest.json` were placed by hand on the shared portraits (runtime pixels in the script's `EYES` table) and checked on `previews/eye-boxes.png`, which draws them over both sets. The SCENE set keeps the same poses, so it uses the same boxes.
