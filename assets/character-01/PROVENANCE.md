# Character Concept Provenance

## Status

The files under `concepts/` are first-party internal concept explorations generated for Project SEAM during the product-design conversation. The files under `blockouts/` were generated by `tools/character-blockout/generate_blockouts.py` from first-party primitive geometry.

## Restrictions

- Internal concept and technical evaluation only.
- Not the final production character.
- Do not represent an approved public trademark, voice-provider likeness, band affiliation, or merchandise design.
- No third-party band logo, album art, voice sample, or character asset is intentionally included.
- A public commercial release requires a final first-party redraw/model, artist agreement, provenance review, and character-IP approval.
